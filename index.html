Using Family Bubbles

  1. Choose a person: Click or tap a bubble once to select it. Click or tap the same bubble again to centre it and open its information panel.
  2. Edit details or add a photo: Change the name, dates, biography or notes, or use Add Photos in the panel to make the newest photograph the portrait while preserving older photographs.
  3. Save before refreshing: Click Save Changes to store your edits, before refreshing. Additional photos are saved when added. Then click the blue ↻ Refresh View button below Delete Person to see updated portraits or lines. Refreshing without saving may lose your edits.
  4. Add family: Click the green Create Person button, enter a name and choose Parent, Partner, Child, Sibling or No relationship. Click Add Person.
  5. Connect existing people: Open a person's panel, use Relationship links to choose another person and the correct relationship, then click Create Relationship Link. Use Refresh View if the line does not appear right away.
  6. Move bubbles: Drag them to reposition them. Selecting a person does not change other people's relative positions.
  7. Back up your family: Use Export Family to download a JSON file with your family information and pictures. Use Import Family to restore it on another browser or device.

Reset: Restores the demo family in this browser, replacing your current family data after confirmation. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.
